I am a DevOps / Cloud Engineer who builds reliable delivery platforms across AWS and GCP. Over the last 5+ years I have focused on making infrastructure predictable with code, tightening feedback loops through CI/CD, and ensuring observability and security are in place from day one.

### Professional Story
- Started with infrastructure automation and evolved into running production workloads on Kubernetes.
- Enabled teams to standardize deployments across frontend and backend services using repeatable pipelines.
- Built guardrails for IAM, cost optimization, and compliance to keep platforms secure and sustainable.

### Engineering Philosophy
- Ship infrastructure as code so environments are reproducible, reviewable, and testable.
- Prefer automation and self-service to reduce operational friction.
- Measure success through reliability, security posture, and cost efficiency.

### Focus Areas
- Platform engineering on AWS and GCP with EKS, GKE, and Autopilot.
- CI/CD automation with GitHub Actions and GitLab.
- Observability stacks with CloudWatch, ELK, Prometheus, and Grafana.
- Security, IAM, and SOC 2-aligned controls embedded into delivery workflows.
